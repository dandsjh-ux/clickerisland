ClickerIsland Wars

Answer island quizzes, build your fortress, then raid rival islands!

❓ How to play
  1. Build phase: earn coins! Quiz answers pay the most (Year 3 → Year 7 pays more). Tapping and Coin Mints help too.
  2. Spend coins on walls, turrets, traps, guard posts and an army of land troops and warships.
  3. PVP phase: press ⚔️ Attack, pick an island, choose a unit and tap the beach (troops) or outer sea (ships) to deploy.
  4. Destroy an enemy Central Command to knock them out. Last island standing wins! If rounds run out, the highest War Score wins.

Desktop: drag to pan, right-drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. Keys: Q quiz, B build, A army, Space tap, 1-4 answer. Mobile: drag to pan, pinch to zoom/rotate.
